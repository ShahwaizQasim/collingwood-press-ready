# Collingwood Press Launch

Build a complete, premium, production-ready landing page for Collingwood Press using the two attached screenshots.

CRITICAL SPLIT:
- Attachment 1 (screencapture-thecollingwoodpress-2026-09-10-20_27_41.png) is the sole CONTENT SOURCE. Use it for all copy, headings, book titles/covers, author testimonials, team members (Daniel Draper, Jay Davis, Alex Brown), services, FAQ questions/answers, and contact details.
- Attachment 2 (screencapture-childrenlp-thecollingwoodpress-2026-09-10-20_25_21.png) is the DESIGN / LAYOUT / THEME SOURCE. Adapt its visual aesthetic—warm cream/pastel backgrounds, bold dark rounded borders, subtle offset shadows, badge pills, organic section rhythms, and editorial typography—into a sophisticated, mature publishing site. Do NOT copy any children's book content, characters, or illustrations.

PAGE STRUCTURE:
1. Header: Sticky responsive navigation with Collingwood Press branding, nav links, and CTA.
2. Hero: "You Wrote 'The Book.' Collingwood Press Gets It Shelf-Ready." with supporting copy, primary/secondary CTAs, and editorial book mockup display.
3. Trust Strip: Platform logos (Gardners, Goodreads, Google, hoopla).
4. Author Trust & Testimonials: "Why Authors Trust Us With Their 'Life's Work'" with carousel/cards, quotes, and author info.
5. Internet Promises Section: "The Internet Is Full Of Promises. Not All Of Them Are Real." with editorial layout and CTA.
6. Book Readiness Section: "Your Book Is Ready. But Does It Look Ready?" featuring styled cards with rounded borders and pastel tints.
7. Cover Design Spotlight: Large book showcase with author/title metadata, description, and CTA.
8. Project Managers: "Meet The Project Managers" (Daniel Draper, Jay Davis, Alex Brown) with professional cards and avatars.
9. Book Showcase: "Collingwood Press Presents" book grid/slider with covers, titles, and descriptions.
10. Social Proof / Extra Reviews: Additional author feedback cards.
11. Major CTA: "Your Story's First Chapter Starts Here." prominent banner with conversion elements.
12. FAQ: Interactive, accessible accordion for all Collingwood Press FAQs.
13. Contact Section: Two-column layout with "Ready to Publish?" inquiry form on the left and "Connect With Us" details on the right.
14. Footer: Dark navy editorial footer with site links, copyright, and publishing credentials.

Ensure full responsiveness (mobile to desktop), Framer Motion animations, accessible controls, and clean component structure.
